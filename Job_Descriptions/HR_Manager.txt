Role: Senior HR Manager & TA Lead
Category: Human Resources
Location: Corporate Office

### Job Summary:
Seeking a Senior HR Manager to lead our talent acquisition and HR operations across a complex corporate group. You will be responsible for managing large-scale F&A teams, oversight of payroll compliance across multiple companies and trusts, and partnering with PE investors on consolidation and restructuring projects.

### Key Responsibilities:
- Lead and manage HR and Talent Acquisition for teams of 30+ employees including qualified professionals across multiple SBUs.
- Partner with C-suite executives and private equity investors (e.g., KKR level) on business consolidation, mergers, and due diligence.
- Formulate and implement group-wide HR policies, compliance frameworks, and performance management systems.
- Manage end-to-end employee lifecycles including large-scale internal communication and change management programs.
- Oversight of statutory audits, legal, and procurement functions as they relate to human resources.

### Requirements & Skills:
- 10+ years of experience in Human Resources management.
- Expertise in full-cycle recruitment, workforce planning, and talent pipe-lining.
- Proficiency in HRIS systems, legal compliance, and procurement functions.
- Strong strategic planning and business partnering capabilities.
- Deep experience in Managing large teams and conflict resolution.

### Education:
- MBA in Human Resources or related Management field.
- Background in leading HR for large MNCs or midsized listed companies is highly desirable.
