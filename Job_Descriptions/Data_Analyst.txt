Role: Senior Data Analyst & BI Specialist
Category: Data Science & Analytics
Location: Remote / Corporate Office

### Job Summary:
Seeking a Senior Data Analyst to lead our business intelligence and strategic reporting initiatives. You will be responsible for transforming large-scale transactional and operational datasets into actionable insights for senior management and stakeholders.

### Key Responsibilities:
- Design and develop sophisticated BI reports providing predictive analytics and performance tracking for retail or similar fast-paced environments.
- Execute ad-hoc data analyses using SQL on Amazon AWS and other cloud data warehousing platforms.
- Architect and implement data pipelines for feature extraction using MapReduce and other big data technologies.
- Define project scopes and collaborate with stakeholders on proof-of-concept machine learning algorithms to address business challenges.
- Transform complex datasets into user-friendly dashboards using Tableau, Power BI, or similar visualization tools.

### Technical Skills & Tools:
- Expert level SQL for data manipulation and querying.
- Mastery of BI visualization tools: Tableau, Power BI, Google Data Studio.
- Specialized Data Tools: MapReduce, Amazon AWS, SQL.
- Strong proficiency in Data Cleaning, Feature Engineering, and Scikit-learn.

### Education & Professional Profile:
- Bachelor's or Master's in Data Science, Statistics, or related quantitative field.
- Proven track record of improving operational accuracy and efficiency by 15%+ through data-driven optimizations.
- Strong ability to present findings to both technical and executive audiences.
