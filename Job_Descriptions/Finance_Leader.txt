Role: Chief Financial Officer (CFO) / Strategic Finance Leader
Category: Executive Finance
Location: Corporate Head Office

### Company Context:
We are a large-scale, multidisciplinary enterprise group with operations across multiple sectors including education, pharma packaging, and renewable energy. We are looking for a Finance Leader to manage our complex corporate ecosystem including multiple companies and trusts.

### Job Summary:
Seeking an elite CFO to lead our financial strategy and governance. In this role, you will be responsible for conceptualizing and formulating financial processes from scratch, formulating compliance frameworks, and leading large-scale fundraising initiatives (both PE and VC). You will oversee multi-location F&A teams and shepherd special projects like mergers and acquisitions.

### Key Responsibilities:
- Managing overall finance functions across multiple companies and trusts with high-volume annual revenues (e.g., Rs. 400+ crore).
- Steering group financial performance and liquidity, managing cash flow effectively in challenging environments.
- Formulating robust cash flow management systems and streamlining pricing/credit control for enterprise businesses.
- Leading F&A teams of 30+ qualified professionals.
- Raising significant working capital funding and term loans (Rs. 50+ crore) from private banks and NBFCs.
- Implementing and overseeing ERP systems (Oracle, Macola) across group entities.

### Technical & Professional Requirements:
- Expert in Strategic & Financial Planning, Financial Consolidation, and Business Partnering.
- In-depth knowledge of Mergers, Acquisitions & Restructuring.
- Mastery of IFRS Standards and GST compliance.
- Six Sigma Green Belt certification is a strong plus.
- Proficiency in Financial Modeling, Risk Management, and Business Analytics.

### Education & Certifications:
- MBA in Finance from a premier institution (IIM or equivalent).
- Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) from a top-tier engineering institute (IIT or equivalent).
- Advanced certifications from Wharton, Harvard Business School, or Columbia Business School are highly desirable.
- Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) designation.
